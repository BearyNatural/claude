Finding the latest version…

Download the app
  1. Open the downloaded file (SowBySeason-….apk) from your notifications or Downloads.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps — this app isn't from the Play Store.
  3. Tap Install. Updating keeps your garden.

Not on Android? Use Sow by Season in your browser — on iPhone, open it in Safari and tap Share › Add to Home Screen.
